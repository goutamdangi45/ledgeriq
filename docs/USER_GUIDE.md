# LedgerIQ User Guide (SRS §2.6)

## Getting started
1. Open the app and choose **Create account**. Enter name, email, a password (8+ characters, with a letter and a number) and your currency.
2. You land on the **Dashboard**. It shows income, expenses, net and total balance for the chosen period, a category chart, a 6-month trend, budget status, savings progress and recent transactions.

## Record money
- **Transactions → + Add transaction**. Choose *Expense* or *Income*, enter an amount greater than zero, a date, a category, payment method and optional notes.
- Use the filter bar to **search** (notes or category) and filter by type, category, method, dates and amount. Click **Export CSV** to download the filtered list.
- **Edit** or **Delete** any of your own transactions; totals update immediately.

## Budgets
- **Budgets → + New budget**. Pick *All spending* for a total budget or a category, set the planned amount and an alert percentage (default 80%).
- Each card shows planned, spent and remaining. You get a notification when spending reaches the alert percentage and again when it passes 100%.

## Savings goals
- **Savings goals → + New goal**. Enter a target (and optionally what you already saved and a target date).
- With a target date, LedgerIQ suggests how much to save per month. Use **Add funds** / **Withdraw** to update progress.

## Reports
- **Reports** summarises any date range: totals, savings rate, spending by category, month-by-month chart, payment methods and your largest expense. **Export CSV** saves the summary.
- **View financial projections →** opens Future prediction.

## Financial advisor
- Gives short suggestions with a **Why am I seeing this?** explanation and a suggested action. If there is too little data it tells you so. Suggestions are general guidance, not professional financial advice.

## Future prediction
- **Next-year budget** – suggested monthly/yearly budget per category from your past spending (adjust the safety buffer and expected price rise; *Use as my budgets* creates real budgets without overwriting existing ones).
- **Balance after 1 year** – estimated balance in 12 months in conservative / expected / optimistic scenarios.
- **Financial projections** – month-by-month chart and table.
- Everything here is an **estimate** based on your recorded history.

## Notifications & profile
- **Notifications** lists budget/goal alerts. **Profile** lets you update your name, currency, notification preferences and password.

## FAQ / troubleshooting
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Please log in to continue" | Your session ended (after 7 days or logout). Log in again. |
| "Too many failed attempts" | Wait 5 minutes and try again. |
| "Choose a valid category" | The category must match the type (income vs expense). |
| "amount must be greater than zero" | Enter a positive number, max 1,000,000,000. |
| Advisor says "Not enough data" | Add at least 8 transactions in the last 3 months. |
| Forecast looks unreliable | It shows its confidence; with under 3 months of history treat it with caution. |
| Network error while saving | Just retry – each Add is protected against duplicates. |
